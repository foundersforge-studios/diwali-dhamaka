Effective Date: November 1, 2026 • Static Entertainment & Fun Game Platform

1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to all visitors of Mega Diwali Dhamaka 2026. It details our non-intrusive data practices, Google AdSense third-party cookie disclosures, browser local storage usage, and user rights under GDPR and CCPA.

3. Information We Collect (Zero Financial Data)

Because our platform is a static novelty simulation with no real money transactions, we collect minimal data:

  • Non-Personal Server Logs: Standard server access logs (IP address, browser type, operating system, timestamp, and referring URL) collected automatically to monitor server health, traffic volume, and bot mitigation.

This data resides exclusively on your personal device and is never sent to or stored on external servers.

4. General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) Compliance

For visitors from the European Economic Area (EEA), we process data in compliance with the EU GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679). You possess the following rights:

  • Right of Access & Portability: You may request confirmation of any data processed and request machine-readable copies.
  • Right to Rectification & Erasure: You may request the update or deletion of your contact records.
  • Right to Object & Restrict Processing: You may object to any analytical processing or cookie placement via the opt-out links above.

To exercise any GDPR rights, please contact our privacy desk at privacy@diwalidhamaka.in.

5. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA) Compliance

For California residents under the CCPA:

  • Do Not Sell Personal Information: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information for monetary value.
  • Non-Discrimination: You will never be denied equal service or functionality for exercising your CCPA rights.

6. Children's Privacy

Our static festive spin-wheel website is family-friendly and non-gambling. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
